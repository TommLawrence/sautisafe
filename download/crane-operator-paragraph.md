## Crane load swing near miss

I was operating the tower crane lifting concrete buckets to the third floor.
The load started swinging because the wind picked up suddenly. The rigger on
the ground was still under the load when it swung. I stopped the hoist
immediately and sounded the horn. The rigger moved away in time. No one was
injured. We stopped work and waited for the wind to calm down before
continuing. The supervisor was informed and we checked the crane's wind speed
indicator - it was reading above the safe limit for lifting.
