# SautiSafe controlled benchmark recordings

These 20 scripts are for one consenting speaker. They are deliberately varied across hazards, negation, equipment, locations, actions, and injury outcomes. Read each script naturally and exactly as written. A pause or repeated word is acceptable, but the verified reference transcript must be edited afterward to match what was actually spoken.

Use participant ID `P11` and filenames `P11-LG-01.wav` through `P11-LG-20.wav`. Select **Luganda-English** when benchmarking. Do not replace Luganda words with an English translation in the verified reference.

## P11-LG-01 - Boiler pressure

Leero ku makya around eight, pressure ku boiler number two yali erinnya mangu. Relief valve yatandise okukuba sound naye tewali muliro. Twakendeeza pressure, ne tuyimiriza boiler, era supervisor n'amanyisibwa. Tewali muntu yafunye injury.

## P11-LG-02 - Conveyor guard

Ku packing line, guard ya conveyor belt yali efulumye mu kifo. Operator yali kumpi okutandika machine naye namugamba ayimirire. Twateeka lockout tag, ne tuyita maintenance. Nobody was injured, era belt teyaddamu kutambula.

## P11-LG-03 - Forklift near miss

Forklift yali edda emabega mu loading bay nga pedestrian ayita emabega waayo. Reverse alarm teyali ekola bulungi. Driver yayimirira just in time, so there was no collision and no injury. Area twagiteekako barrier ne tuyita supervisor.

## P11-LG-04 - Chemical splash

Mu mixing room, hose ya chemical pump yavaako ne small amount n'esansukira ku glove y'omukozi. Teyatuuka ku skin era tewali burn. Twaggala pump, ne tunaaza area, then safety officer n'akebera PPE n'okuyunga hose.

## P11-LG-05 - Wet floor

Water yali esaze ku floor okumpi ne electrical panel mu utility room. No one slipped and the panel was not wet. Twaziyiza abantu okuyitawo, ne tusiba source ya leak, era electrician n'akebera area before reopening.

## P11-LG-06 - Crane load swing

Tower crane yali esitula concrete bucket nga wind yeeyongera. Load yatandise okuswinga era rigger yali kumpi nayo. Operator yayimiriza hoist n'akuba horn; rigger n'avaawo safely. Tewali injury, era lifting yayimirizibwa until wind speed yakka.

## P11-LG-07 - Gas smell

Twawulira smell ya gas mu cylinder store naye tetwalaba flame oba smoke. Valve ku cylinder emu yali loose. Twaggya abantu mu room, ne tuggala main valve, then emergency team n'ekebera concentration. Nobody was injured.

## P11-LG-08 - Scaffold board

Ku second floor, scaffold board emu yali cracked era ng'etandise okuseeseetuka. Worker teyagirinnyako. Twateeka tag nti do not use, ne tuggyako access ladder, era competent person n'ajja okugikyusa. There was no fall.

## P11-LG-09 - Grinder sparks

Worker yali akozesa angle grinder mu workshop nga sparks zigenda okumpi ne oily rags. There was no fire. Namuyimiriza, ne tuggyawo combustible materials, ne tukebera fire extinguisher before work resumed. PPE ye yali complete.

## P11-LG-10 - Minor hand cut

Mu fabrication area, sharp edge ku metal sheet yasala worker ku finger. Cut yali minor naye blood yatono. Machine yayimirizibwa, first aider n'anaaza wound n'agiteekako dressing. The worker remained stable and supervisor recorded the incident.

## P11-LG-11 - Pump vibration

Cooling-water pump number three yatandise okuvibrate nnyo era bearing n'ewulikika nga ekuba. Temperature yali erinnya naye tewali smoke. Twakyusa flow ku standby pump, ne tu-isolate unit, then maintenance n'etandika inspection.

## P11-LG-12 - Electrical cable

Extension cable mu maintenance bay yali eyonoonese insulation nga copper erabika. Nobody touched the exposed section and there was no shock. Power twagiggyako, ne tuteeka cable mu quarantine, era electrician n'agikyusa.

## P11-LG-13 - Falling tool

Spanner yagudde okuva ku platform n'egwa mu exclusion zone. Tewali muntu yali wansi era nobody was struck. Twagaziya barricade, ne tukebera tool lanyards, then team n'ekubirizibwa okusiba tools before continuing.

## P11-LG-14 - Steam leak

Small steam leak yalabise ku flange mu boiler house. Area yali eyokya nnyo naye tewali muntu yayidde. Twaziyiza access, ne tukendeeza pressure, era line n'eggalwa. Maintenance waited for isolation confirmation before approaching.

## P11-LG-15 - Blocked emergency exit

Pallets bbiri zaali ziteekeddwa mu maaso ga emergency exit mu warehouse. There was no emergency at the time, but the escape route was blocked. Twaziggyawo immediately, ne tuteeka floor markings, era warehouse lead n'ayogera ne team.

## P11-LG-16 - Vehicle brake warning

Site pickup yalaze brake warning light nga driver agenda okufuluma gate. Driver teyagenda ku road. Vehicle yayimirizibwa mu safe parking area, keys ne ziggyibwako, then mechanic n'ayitibwa. No accident and no one was injured.

## P11-LG-17 - Dust exposure

Dust collector mu bagging section yali stopped era dust nnyingi yatandise okubeera mu mpewo. Workers baali bambadde masks naye visibility yali ekendeera. Twaggala filling machine, ne tuggya abantu mu section, era ventilation n'ekeberwa.

## P11-LG-18 - Ladder instability

Portable ladder yali etegeereddwa ku uneven ground nga worker agenda okulinnya. Omukozi teyagituukako kubanga colleague yamulabula. Twagiggyawo, ne tuleeta platform ladder entuufu, era ground ne tukeberwa. There was no fall or injury.

## P11-LG-19 - Hot bearing

Bearing ku conveyor drive yali eyokya era temperature alarm n'ekuba. There was no flame and no smoke. Operator yayimiriza conveyor using the normal stop, then power n'e-isolatebwa. Maintenance found low lubrication and began repair.

## P11-LG-20 - Confined-space control

Contractor yali agenda okuyingira mu tank nga gas test certificate tennakakasibwa. Entry teyatandika. Supervisor yayimiriza omulimu, ne tukebera permit, ventilation, rescue equipment, and standby person. No exposure occurred and nobody entered the tank.

## Recording checklist

- Record in a quiet but realistic room using the same phone and normal speaking distance.
- Do not rehearse until the delivery becomes unnaturally perfect.
- Submit each recording as a separate report.
- In supervisor review, listen back and edit the transcript to the exact spoken words.
- Save the verified transcript before pressing **Benchmark this report**.
- Benchmark once after verification. Record failed provider calls; do not silently rerun only one model.
