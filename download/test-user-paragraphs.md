# SautiSafe test-user paragraphs (10)

Read one of these paragraphs aloud in the SautiSafe Report tab. As you read,
mix in some Luganda words where it feels natural - just like you would on a
real site. The app transcribes code-switched speech.

Pick the Luganda-English speaking language in the Report tab before you
record. Enter your name in "Your name" so the supervisor sees who reported.

---

## 1. Boiler pressure relief (near miss)

This morning around nine, the pressure on reactor two was going up too high.
The relief valve started making a sound, but we managed to bring it down.
There was no fire and no one was injured. It happened at the boiler house. We
isolated the line and called the supervisor.

## 2. Chemical spill at the drum storage

There was a chemical spill near the drum storage in the warehouse. Fumes were
coming out. We isolated the area and called the safety officer. Nobody was
hurt. The spill was contained with absorbent material and the area was
ventilated.

## 3. Forklift near miss on the production line

A forklift was moving too fast near the loading bay. The operator did not see
a worker walking across. We told the operator to stop immediately. No injury
happened. We have asked the operator to slow down and use the horn at
corners.

## 4. Electrical arc flash on the isolator

There was an arc flash on the isolator while maintenance was working on the
panel. One worker got a minor burn on the hand. We shut down and locked out
the panel. The worker was taken to the clinic for first aid.

## 5. Scaffold collapse near the east wall

A scaffold collapsed near the east wall of the construction site. Two workers
were nearby but no serious injury. We cordoned off the area and called the
supervisor. The scaffold was inspected and found to have a loose joint.

## 6. Gas leak in the compressor room

We smelt gas in the compressor room. We evacuated the area and shut off the
main valve. No one was injured. The gas company was called to inspect the
line. The room was ventilated before anyone went back in.

## 7. Conveyor belt entanglement (near miss)

A worker's sleeve got caught in the conveyor belt at the packing line. He
pulled free before any injury. We stopped the line and reported it. The guard
over the belt was found missing and has been replaced.

## 8. Hot surface burn (minor injury)

A worker touched a hot steam pipe near the boiler and got a minor burn on the
forearm. We gave first aid and sent him to the clinic. The pipe has now been
lagged with insulation. No one else was affected.

## 9. Slipped on a wet floor (no injury)

I slipped on a wet floor near the washing station. I did not fall and there
was no injury. The floor had no warning sign. We have now placed a wet-floor
sign and cleaned up the spill.

## 10. Uncontrolled pressure release (urgent)

The pressure on the hydraulic line suddenly released and oil sprayed out near
the pump. We hit the emergency stop and isolated the line. No one was injured
but it was a serious near miss. The relief valve was found stuck and has been
replaced.

## Tips for mixing in Luganda

You do not have to translate everything. Just swap in a Luganda word or
phrase where it feels natural, for example:

- "The pressure ebadde egenda waggulu" (was going up too high)
- "Naye twasobodde okugikendeeza" (but we managed to bring it down)
- "Hakuna majeraha" (no injuries - this is Swahili, also fine)
- "Tuliabudde operator a-stop" (we told the operator to stop)
- "Twatadde ku line" (we isolated the line)

Speak at your normal pace. The app is built for code-switched speech.
